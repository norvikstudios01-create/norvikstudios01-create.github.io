Privacy Policy for Norvik Studios Norvik Studios operates the mobile games. This Privacy Policy Website explains how we handle information when you use our App. 1. Information We Collect 1.1 Information You Provide We do not directly collect any personal information from you. The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal details to use its core features. 1.2 Game Center Integration Our App integrates with Apple's Game Center service. When you sign in to Game Center, Apple may collect and process information according to their privacy policy. We do not have access to your Game Center personal information. Please review Apple's privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ for more information. 1.3 Automatically Collected Information Through our third-party service providers (Facebook SDK and Google AdMob), the following information may be automatically collected: Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, mobile network information Advertising Identifiers: Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) if you consent to tracking Usage Data: App launches, session duration, in-app events (such as level completion, game achievements) Location Data: Approximate location (country/city level) based on IP address Performance Data: Crash reports, app performance metrics 2. How We Use Your Information The information collected through third-party services is used to: Deliver personalized advertisements within the App Measure and analyze advertising campaign effectiveness Improve app performance and user experience Understand user behavior and app usage patterns Diagnose technical issues and crashes 3. In-App Purchases Our App offers in-app purchases to acquire game coins and remove advertisements. All in-app purchase transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs the collection and use of payment-related information. When you make an in-app purchase, Facebook SDK may record this event (without payment details) for advertising attribution purposes. 4. Data Storage The App stores your game progress, preferences, and settings locally on your device. This data includes: Game progress and scores In-game currency (coins) balance Game settings and preferences Daily reward streak information Tutorial completion status This information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. 5. Advertising and Third-Party Services 5.1 Google AdMob We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in our App. AdMob may collect and use data to provide personalized ads. AdMob may collect: Device identifiers and advertising IDs IP address and approximate location Ad interaction data Device and app usage information Learn more about Google's privacy practices: https://policies.google.com/privacy Opt out of personalized ads: https://adssettings.google.com 5.2 Facebook SDK (Meta) We use Facebook SDK to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns. Facebook SDK may collect: Device identifiers and advertising IDs App install and app open events In-app events (level completion, purchases, achievements) Device information and IP address This data helps us understand which advertising campaigns are effective and optimize our marketing efforts. Learn more about Facebook's privacy practices: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/ 5.3 Data Sharing with Third Parties We share data with the following third-party service providers: Meta Platforms Inc. (Facebook): For advertising attribution and campaign analytics Google LLC (AdMob): For serving advertisements Apple Inc. (Game Center, In-App Purchases): For gaming services and payment processing These third parties may combine data collected from our App with data from other sources to provide their services. 5.4 Your Choices Regarding Tracking iOS 14.5 and later: When you first launch the App, you will see a prompt asking for permission to track you across apps and websites. You can choose to: "Allow": Permit the App to use your IDFA for personalized advertising and attribution "Ask App Not to Track": Prevent the App from accessing your IDFA You can change this choice at any time in: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking Note: Choosing "Ask App Not to Track" will limit personalized advertising but will not prevent ads from being shown. You will still see ads, but they may be less relevant to you. 6. Children's Privacy Our App is rated 4+ and is suitable for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The App does not require any personal information to be used. However, our third-party advertising and analytics partners (Google AdMob and Facebook SDK) may collect data as described in this policy. Parents and guardians should: Review this Privacy Policy and our third-party partners' privacy policies Use iOS parental controls to manage app tracking permissions Use iOS restrictions to manage in-app purchases Supervise children's use of the App 7. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect data transmitted to our third-party partners. However, please note: Game data stored locally on your device depends on your device's security Data transmitted to third-party services is subject to their security practices No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure We recommend that you: Keep your device's operating system up to date Use device security features such as passcodes or biometric authentication Be cautious about who has access to your device Review your iOS privacy settings regularly 8. Data Retention Your game data stored locally remains on your device until you delete the App or manually clear the App's data. We do not retain any of your personal data on our servers. Data collected by our third-party partners (Google AdMob, Facebook SDK) is retained according to their respective privacy policies and data retention practices. 9. Your Rights 9.1 Access and Control You have the right to: Opt out of tracking: Use iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking Limit ad tracking: Disable personalized ads in iOS settings Delete local data: Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data Request information: Contact us to learn about data our third-party partners may have collected 9.2 Managing Google AdMob Data Visit https://myadcenter.google.com to: Control ad personalization See what data Google uses for ads Opt out of personalized advertising 9.3 Managing Facebook Data Visit https://www.facebook.com/ads/preferences to manage your Facebook ad preferences. 10. Data collected by our third-party partners (Facebook and Google) may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States. These countries may have different data protection laws than your country. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your data to these countries. 11.
